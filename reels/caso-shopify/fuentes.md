    https://www.shopify.com/blog/earliest-businesses

  - Shopify, "Earliest Merchant Stories" ("they decided to create their own"):
    https://www.shopify.com/blog/earliest-businesses
  - Independiente: NPR, How I Built This: https://www.npr.org/2019/08/02/747660923/shopify-tobias-l-tke
